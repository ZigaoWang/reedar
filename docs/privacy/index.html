Support Privacy

Reedar does not collect any data about you.

That is the whole policy.

Everything below is only what it means in practice. Last updated 9 August 2026.

What Reedar stores

Everything you enter, meaning your reeds, your sessions and your notes, is stored on your device in the app's own database. There is no account to create and nothing to sign in to.

What Reedar sends

Nothing. Reedar has no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no third-party SDKs, and makes no network requests of its own.

iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, iOS may sync Reedar's database between your own devices through your personal iCloud account. That data belongs to you, is covered by Apple's privacy policy, and is not visible to the developer. You can stop it at any time in Settings → Apple Account → iCloud.

Tracking

Reedar does not track you across apps or websites, and does not use the Advertising Identifier.

Children

Reedar collects nothing from anyone, of any age.

Links out of the app

The About screen links to the developer's website and to the app's GitHub repository. Following one of those takes you to a site with its own privacy policy.

This website

reedar.app is a handful of static files. There are no cookies, no analytics and no trackers on it. It is served by GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
